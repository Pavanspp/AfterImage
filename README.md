About

Everything you do is recorded. 1.2 seconds later, a ghost replays your exact movement. You have two abilities:

Freeze — locks the echo in place. It becomes solid geometry. You can stand on it, wall jump off it, use it as a platform anywhere in the level.

Teleport — moves you instantly to wherever the echo currently is. If the echo is frozen, teleporting to it unfreezes it and it starts following you again.



Controls

Arrow keys or WASD — move 

Space — jump (hold for higher, tap for short hop) 

Left Click — freeze / unfreeze echo 

Right Click — teleport to echo 

Escape — pause menu
